Basecamp + HEY

Work stuff and life stuff, on one week.

I use Basecamp for work and HEY for everything else, and I wanted one place to plan both. Some days are mostly work, some are mostly personal, most are a mix. Daybreak puts it all on the same week, so I can plan the day I actually have.

Kosta, who made it

BasecampYour work stuff

Comes in

  • To-dos assigned to you
  • Schedule entries
  • Cards from your card tables
  • Notes and comments, so you don't have to go digging

Goes back

  • Check off a to-do and it's done in Basecamp
  • Finish a card and it moves to Done

HEYYour personal stuff Optional

Comes in

  • Your HEY Calendar, next to your to-dos
  • Imbox, Reply Later and Set Aside
  • Turn any email into a to-do

Goes back

  • Block time for a to-do and it shows up on your calendar
  • Your evening note goes to your HEY Journal

The week

See your whole week at once.

Every day fits on the screen. Drag a to-do from Tuesday to Thursday. Not sure when? Drop it in Sometime this week and decide later.

  • Stamp it done. Finishing something should feel good. Pick the stamp you like.
  • Set goals, keep a log. Three goals for the week and a quick note for each day.
  • Works on your phone. One day at a time, with everything a thumb away.

How it's built

Simple, private, and yours.

  • Just for you

    One install, one person. Your team keeps using Basecamp like always. Daybreak never bugs them.

  • Quiet

    No notifications, no badges, no streaks. It's there when you open it.

  • Private

    Everything lives in one file on your server. No tracking in the app or on this site.

  • Open source

    Free and MIT licensed. Change whatever you want, and keep it forever.

Self-host

Run it yourself.

It's one Rails app and one SQLite file. A laptop, a $5 server, or Railway all work fine.

With Docker

  1. Create a Basecamp integration

    At launchpad.37signals.com/integrations, with the redirect URL http://localhost:3000/auth/basecamp/callback.

  2. Clone, add its keys to .env, and start 
    git clone https://github.com/is2b007/daybreak.git
cd daybreak && cp .env.example .env
docker compose up -d
  3. Open localhost:3000

    Onboarding connects your accounts. HEY is optional.

Or in one click

Deploy to Railway for about $5 a month. Set RAILS_MASTER_KEY, BASECAMP_CLIENT_ID and BASECAMP_CLIENT_SECRET, and add a volume at /rails/storage so your data survives deploys.
