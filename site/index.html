Two apps, one week

Everything you owe, in the order you'll do it.

Daybreak doesn't replace Basecamp or HEY. It reads what's on your plate from both, lets you decide when each thing happens, and writes the result back where it belongs.

Basecamp

  • Assignments and schedule entries come in on their own, ready to drag onto a day.
  • Card table cards show their stage, and finishing one moves it to Done.
  • Check off a to-do in Daybreak and it's checked off in Basecamp.
  • Descriptions and comments open right on the card, rich text and all.

HEY Optional

  • Your calendar sits beside your to-dos, and a timebox becomes a HEY event, reminders intact.
  • Imbox, Reply Later and Set Aside in a side panel, and any email can become a to-do.
  • Your sundown note lands in the HEY Journal, so the day is written down where you'll find it.

The week

A week you can see all of.

Every day fits on screen at any width, so nothing hides off to the right. Drag work between days. Things without a day wait in Sometime this week along the bottom.

  • Finish with a stamp. Done gets an ink stamp, the way a paper planner would.
  • Made for your phone too. One day at a time, with Week, Today, Inbox and Log a thumb away.
  • Weekly goals and a daily log. Three goals for the week and a running note for each day.

Rituals

Start slowly. Stop on purpose.

Two short rituals bookend the day, each under its own sky. They take a couple of minutes, and they're the part people miss when they go back to a plain to-do list.

Sunrise · 7:42

Good morning, Kosta.

A short look back at yesterday, then you choose what today is for. Carry over what didn't get done, pull in what's due, and set a timebox or two.

  1. Look back
  2. Pick today's work
  3. Timebox it

Sundown · 18:05

How did today go?

Tick off what you finished, move what you didn't, and write a line or two about the day. With HEY connected, that note goes straight to your Journal.

Journal · Oct 21

Roadmap's drafted. The design review ran long, but it was worth it.

Both rituals have an optional soft soundtrack, made live in your browser. Set up takes one long sunrise too: onboarding walks you from night to morning while you connect Basecamp and HEY.

Focus

One thing, nothing else.

Open any to-do in Focus and the rest of the week goes away. A timer, your private notes, and the Basecamp description and comments if you need them.

SilenceRainWavesDrift

Four calm sounds, generated as you listen. Nothing to download, nothing streamed.

On purpose

What Daybreak won't do.

  • No team features. It's your plan. Basecamp is where the team works.
  • No AI. You decide what today is for.
  • No notifications. Daybreak waits until you open it.
  • No analytics. Not in the app, not on this page.

Self-host

Yours, on your own server.

Daybreak is one Rails app with one SQLite file. Run it on a laptop, a $5 box, or Railway in a click.

Docker

git clone https://github.com/is2b007/daybreak.git
cd daybreak
cp .env.example .env
docker compose up -d
open http://localhost:3000

Add your Basecamp client ID and secret to .env first.

Railway

One click, about $5 a month. Add a volume at /rails/storage so your data survives deploys.

Deploy on Railway

You'll need

  • A Basecamp integration from launchpad.37signals.com
  • RAILS_MASTER_KEY, BASECAMP_CLIENT_ID, BASECAMP_CLIENT_SECRET
  • A HEY account, if you want calendar, email and journal
